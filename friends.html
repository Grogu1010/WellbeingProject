Being With Friends

People make the good times even better

Spending quality time with friends keeps my energy up and reminds me that support is always nearby. Here are some of the moments that have boosted my wellbeing lately.

Neither were as scary as we were hoping for but I had a good time and I think that everyone else did too. We’re already planning to watch the sequel.

Lunchtime Bowling

Yesterday at lunch I went bowling with Toby and Adelle. Like, cricket bowling. I don’t really like sport and so didn’t like the physical aspect but I just liked doing something with the people that I like to spend time with and just chill out with. And then we played catch which was just fun, so, yeah.
