Being With Friends

People make the good times even better

Spending quality time with friends keeps my energy up and reminds me that support is always nearby. Here are some of the moments that have boosted my wellbeing lately.

Halloween Hangout

Escape room + spooky movie night

Halloween was less about jump scares and more about bonding. Felipe, Toby and Adelle came over, we cracked puzzles in an escape room and watched IT (2017). Even though it wasn't terrifying, it was a brilliant night together.

Already planning to watch the sequel!

Lunch break adventures

Trying cricket bowling (and catch!)

We tried cricket bowling at lunch. I’m not the biggest sports fan, but doing it with Toby and Adelle made it fun. We finished the session with a game of catch and plenty of laughs.

Quality time beats perfect technique.
